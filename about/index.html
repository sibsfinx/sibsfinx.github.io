About Alexander Mescheryakov

Alexander Mescheryakov is a UX and UI designer and front-end developer — also known online as sibsfinx. He designs and builds digital products, then improves experiences and interfaces so they match real user needs and business goals with as little waste as possible.

His practice sits between product design and production UI: UX flows, interface systems, design-to-code collaboration, and front-end implementation. He has shipped with remote product teams in the US, UK, Germany, and Canada across SaaS, healthcare, construction tech, and knowledge platforms.

How he works

Engagements usually start from a concrete product surface — activation, a dense B2B workflow, a design system that must survive production, or a marketing site that needs to become a product. He prefers tight feedback loops, explicit constraints, and decisions that reduce rework for design and engineering teammates.

He moves between Figma-level interface design and modern front-end implementation, documenting rationale so other people can continue the system. The goal is a usable product, not maximal process.

Focus areas

  • UX design: flows, information architecture, interaction design
  • UI design: visual systems, components, responsive layouts
  • Front-end development: HTML, CSS, JavaScript/TypeScript, component-driven UI
  • Product collaboration: scoping, prioritization, shipping judgment
