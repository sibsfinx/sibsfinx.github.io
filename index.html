About Alexander Mescheryakov

Alexander Mescheryakov (also known as sibsfinx) is a product engineer who specializes in frontend engineering, UX and UI design, and product design, with additional full-stack and product-management experience. He helps teams turn ambiguous product goals into clear interfaces, resilient front-end implementations, and measurable experience improvements without unnecessary process or rework.

Typical engagements include discovery and UX flows, interface systems, design-to-code handoff, front-end architecture, accessibility-minded UI implementation, and iterative shipping with designers, engineers, and stakeholders. The working principle is simple: match user needs and business goals with minimum waste.

When to work with Alexander

  • You need a senior generalist who can move between research, UI design, and production front-end code.
  • You want to improve an existing product experience without a large redesign theater.
  • You need portfolio-quality UI craft plus practical engineering judgment on web and mobile web surfaces.
