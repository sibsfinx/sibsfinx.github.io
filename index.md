# Alexander Mescheryakov

I'm Alexander Mescheryakov, a Lead / Senior Software Engineer with 15 years of experience building and scaling SaaS and e-commerce products. I focus on business needs: clarifying requirements, planning and evaluating projects, leading engineering teams, and coordinating external partners. I've worked across e-commerce and marketing analytics, 3D/CAD/AR collaboration, project management, security and video, and healthcare — including US healthcare/insurance and UK healthcare/wellness/performance. A strong part of my work is supporting sales and marketing — reliable client-facing product, faster release cycles, and outcomes that help go-to-market and growth.

As a team lead and hands-on engineer, I own delivery end to end while managing stakeholders and external teams. At Littledata, I rebuilt a Shopify data-layer product for analytics and multi-channel advertising — reducing client-facing production issues to zero and shortening the design, engineering, and QA cycle by approximately 50%. At Vectary, I led engineering on a 3D/CAD/AR collaboration platform that improved application loading by 10× and monorepo builds by 20×. Earlier product engineering includes Fohlio and IQ300 growth outcomes, plus work with teams including 3dEYE, US healthcare/insurance, and UK healthcare/wellness/performance.
